MediCounter

MediCounter is a Windows app for managing medication stock per person. It is built for stock organization, not intake tracking, and keeps data local on the device.

Product

Medication stock management for Windows

MediCounter is meant for organizing existing medication stock per person. The current beta focuses on a clear start screen, simple stock management, and a setup that works without registration or cloud sync.

Description

What MediCounter is

  • Medication stock management per person
  • Built for local desktop use on Windows
  • Designed to stay simple and readable
  • Works without an account or cloud connection

Not Included

What MediCounter does not do

  • No intake reminders or dose scheduling
  • No medical recommendations
  • No diagnosis or treatment guidance
  • No automatic online sync
